Last Modified: February 21, 2026

1. Privacy Policy & Data Handling

College Night Live ("we," "us," or "our") values your privacy. We use Google Authentication to prevent bots and platform abuse.

  • Information We Collect: Your name and email address provided via Google Sign-In, and any content you voluntarily post to the Intel Feed.
  • Usage: We use this data to manage your account and attribute reports. We do not sell or rent user data to third parties.
  • Cookies: We use essential session cookies through Firebase to keep you logged into the platform.

Strict Non-Sharing Clause: No personal user information—including email addresses or names—will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

2. Intel Feed & Conduct

The Intel Feed is a public forum for campus updates. By using this feature, you agree to the following:

  • Public Visibility: Anything you post is visible to all registered users at your university.
  • Moderation: We reserve the right to remove any content that is harassing, illegal, or violates campus safety standards.
  • Responsibility: You are solely responsible for the content you post.

3. Wait-Time Algorithm Disclaimer

CollegeNightLive is an information-sharing tool. Wait times displayed are estimates calculated using a consensus-weighted algorithm based on live user reports and historical baselines.

Accuracy Notice: Real-world conditions change rapidly. We do not guarantee the accuracy of wait times. Use of this data is at your own risk, and we are not liable for any inconvenience or outcome resulting from inaccurate estimates.
