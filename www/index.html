PROTECTING THE VIBE

CollegeNightLife is not here to create a system where you're required to pay to enjoy the college experience like a Rival Company have done at schools like Penn State. We believe the best memories are those that are made with your friends for free.

What we would provide an option for is a 1-Night Pass to skip lines throughout the night. This is designed for students celebrating special occasions with their friends like a birthday to maximize the memories you make during your special day.

To ensure we don't monopolize the market like they have done, we only issue 50 Unlimited Skip Passes per night for a school like Ohio State. This ensures those who skip never negatively impact the wait times for everyone else.
